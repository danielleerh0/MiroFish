# Situation Briefing: Kestrel Park Cold-Chain Disruption (SYNTHETIC TEST FIXTURE)

All organisations, assets and figures in this briefing are fictional. It exists to test
ScenarioIQ extraction and the deterministic engine.

## Situation

A power fault at the Kestrel Park medical store requires the Medical Logistics Authority
to relocate its temperature-controlled stock. The medical inventory at Kestrel Park is 480 pallets.
Once removed from the cold room, the stock has a cold-hold limit of 6 hours.

All outbound pallets must pass through Dock 1. Dock 1 handles 40 pallets per hour.

## Transport assets

Tidewater Logistics owns refrigerated truck RT-05. RT-05 carries 16 pallets.
RT-05 is currently committed to Greenmart for its morning grocery run.
RT-05 is also listed as standby capacity for Linkhaul. RT-05 is parked at Kestrel Park.

Quayline Carriers operates refrigerated truck QL-12, which carries 20 pallets and is currently unassigned.
Quayline's Good Distribution Practice certification expired on 15 September 2026.
Tidewater's Good Distribution Practice certification is valid.
Only vehicles run by an operator with valid Good Distribution Practice certification may carry medical stock.

## Downstream dependencies

Greenmart supplies the Corner Basket store network. Corner Basket can only receive deliveries between 06:00 and 09:00.

## Information picture

The Medical Logistics Authority has not been told that RT-05 is committed to Greenmart, and still
treats it as available. The Authority is also unaware that Quayline's certification has lapsed.
Greenmart knows its RT-05 booking is active.
